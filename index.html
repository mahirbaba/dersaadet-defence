How to fight

  • Pick a unit in the dock, then tap a courtyard tile next to the road. Tap twice on touch screens to confirm.
  • Press Savaş! to send the next wave. Call it early for bonus akçe.
  • Tap a tower to upgrade it to level 3 or sell it. Mehter makes neighbours fire faster.
  • Pavise men shrug off arrows. Bring janissaries and the Şahi bombard.
  • Rain drops a volley wherever you tap. Kös drives every tower faster for 8 seconds.
  • The cats of the city are friendly. Win with no losses for three stars.
Progress saves after every cleared wave.
